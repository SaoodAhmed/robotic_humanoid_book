## Version Information

### Isaac Sim
- **Current Version**: 2023.1+ (as of documentation)
- **ROS 2 Distribution**: Compatible with ROS 2 Humble Hawksbill
- **CUDA Requirement**: CUDA 11.8 or newer

### Isaac ROS
- **Current Version**: ROS 2 Humble compatible packages
- **Release Channel**: ROS 2 package repositories
- **Dependencies**: ROS 2 Humble, CUDA, TensorRT

### Nav2
- **Current Version**: ROS 2 Humble compatible
- **Package Version**: 1.2.x series for Humble
- **Dependencies**: ROS 2 Humble, TF2, Costmap 2D
